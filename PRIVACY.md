# Privacy Policy — Proctor Exam Monitoring Extension

**Effective date:** 9 October 2026

Proctor is a browser extension used by educational institutions to support
academic integrity during online exams. This policy explains exactly what the
extension does and does not collect. It is written to match the extension's
actual behaviour.

> **In short:** Proctor only monitors your activity **while you are joined to an
> exam**. It records that you switched tabs, changed windows, navigated to a
> page, or used copy/paste — **not** what you type, and **not** the contents of
> what you copy. Nothing is collected before you join or after you leave.

## When monitoring is active

The extension collects and transmits data **only while you are joined to an
exam**. You join by opening the extension popup and entering a join code
provided by your instructor. Monitoring stops when you choose **Leave exam** or
**Finish**, when the instructor closes the exam, or when the session ends. Before
you join, the extension does not collect or transmit any data.

To join, you must allow the extension in **incognito** windows (Chrome →
Extensions → Proctor → Details → *Allow in Incognito*). During an exam, activity
in incognito windows is monitored in the same way as in normal windows; outside
an exam nothing is collected in either.

## What we collect

While you are joined to an exam:

- **Information you provide:** your name, your student ID, and the join code
  you enter.
- **Monitoring events**, each with a timestamp:
  - switching between browser tabs, and the URL of the tab you switch to;
  - opening, closing, or navigating a tab, and the URL involved;
  - the browser window losing or gaining focus (e.g. switching to another app);
  - opening a new browser window, including whether it is an incognito window;
  - clipboard actions — that a **copy, paste, or cut occurred**, and the URL of
    the page — but **not** the text involved;
  - leaving fullscreen on the exam page, and periods of inactivity (only the
    idle/active/locked state, never any input);
  - reaching the exam form's "response recorded" page (to know you submitted);
  - turning off the extension's incognito access during an exam.
- **Technical information:** your browser's user-agent string and the
  extension's version number.

## What we do NOT collect

- **No keystroke logging.** We do not record what you type, anywhere.
- **No mouse tracking.** We do not record mouse movement or position.
- **No clipboard contents.** We record that you copied or pasted, never the text.
- **No page content, form values, or passwords.**
- **No screenshots, camera, microphone, or audio.**
- **No browsing activity outside an exam** — nothing before you join or after
  you leave.
- We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with any
  third party.

## Where your data goes

Monitoring events are sent to a server operated by your educational institution
(your instructor or university). Proctor is self-hosted software: the developers
do not receive, store, or have access to your data. All questions about how your
institution stores, uses, or retains this data — and any request to access or
delete it — should be directed to your instructor or institution.

## Data controller & retention

Your educational institution is the controller of the data collected during
exams and determines how long it is retained. Proctor provides the software; it
does not operate a central service.

## Permissions the extension uses

- **Tabs / Windows:** to detect tab switches, new windows and window focus changes during an exam.
- **Storage:** to remember your current exam session (and, until you join, what
  you typed in the join form) locally on your device.
- **Alarms:** to send a periodic "still active" signal to the exam server.
- **Idle:** to notice periods of inactivity during an exam (state only).
- **Notifications / Scripting:** to show you warnings during an exam, as a
  desktop notification and as a banner on the page you are viewing. Nothing is
  read from the page.
- **Host access:** to detect navigation and clipboard actions on the pages you
  visit during an exam.
